# Curated Recovery Store Plan

Quiet Hour's future shop should feel like a restrained extension of the directory: useful recovery objects chosen for a clear use case, not a sprawling dropshipping catalogue. Begin only after the directory has meaningful visitor demand and the team can assess supplier reliability, delivery times, product quality, and returns.

| Initial collection | Customer job | Supplier diligence before listing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Massage oils and simple balms | At-home self-massage or practitioner-adjacent care | Ingredient list, allergen details, local compliance, packaging quality. |
| Heat wraps and recovery textiles | Comfort after walking, travel, or a workday | Electrical-safety status where applicable, material specification, care instructions. |
| Mobility and self-massage tools | Gentle, practical recovery routines | Durable construction, plain-language use instructions, injury-risk cautions. |
| Travel recovery essentials | Light, useful products for visitors | Compact shipping, consistent stock, clear international delivery terms. |

No product should be added merely because a supplier makes it available. The initial release should cap the range, use real product information, set honest delivery expectations, and state contraindications where a product could be unsuitable. Customer reviews, ratings, and performance claims must never be fabricated.
